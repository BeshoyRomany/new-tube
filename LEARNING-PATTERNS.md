# Learning System Notes

## Core Concepts

### Deep Processing — المعالجة العميقة

Understanding ideas at a conceptual level by connecting them to meaning, not just memorizing.

---

### Cognitive Load — الحمل المعرفي

The amount of mental effort your brain can handle at one time before slowing down.

---

### Spaced Repetition — التكرار المتباعد

Reviewing information over time in intervals to strengthen long-term memory.

---

### Deliberate Practice — الممارسة المتعمدة

Focused practice on weak points with clear intention to improve specific skills.

---

### Pattern Recognition — التعرف على الأنماط

The brain’s ability to recognize repeated structures and reuse them automatically.

---

### Incremental Learning — التعلم التدريجي

Building understanding step by step in layers instead of all at once.

---

## Learning Rules (Personal System)

- Move forward even if understanding is incomplete
- Limit deep dives (10–15 minutes max)
- Mark unclear points and revisit later
- Focus on patterns, not every line of code
- Learn concepts separately, then reconnect them to code
